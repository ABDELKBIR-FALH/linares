Frequently Asked Questions

Find all the essential information you need in our FAQ section, designed to address the most frequently asked questions.

What are the school hours at Little Learners Academy?

Our school hours are from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday. We also offer extended care options for parents who need early drop-off or late pick-up.

How do you handle food allergies and dietary restrictions?

We keep a detailed record of every child's allergies and dietary needs.

Is there a uniform policy for students?

Yes, students wear a simple school uniform. Details are shared during admission.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio at Little Learners Academy?

Our small class sizes allow every child to receive personalized attention.

What extracurricular activities are available for students?

We offer arts and crafts, sports, music, and social events.

How do you handle discipline and behavior management?

We use positive reinforcement and gentle guidance to help children learn respect and self-control.

How do I apply for admission to Little Learners Academy?

Fill in the application form, book a school visit, and our team will guide you.
